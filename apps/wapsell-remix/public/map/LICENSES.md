# Fonts and icons of Wapsell's map (OR-B4-054)

Copied unchanged from [protomaps/basemaps-assets](https://github.com/protomaps/basemaps-assets)
at commit `028c18f713baecad011301ff7a69acc39bcc2ae7`. The app serves them itself, so the map
does not depend on anyone else's server.

- `fonts/`: Noto Sans Regular, Medium and Italic, glyph ranges 0-255, 256-511, 512-767,
  768-1023, 7680-7935 and 8192-8447 (Latin, its accents, and punctuation). A character outside
  them is drawn with the browser's own font. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1, in
  [`fonts/OFL.txt`](fonts/OFL.txt).
- `sprites/v4/`: the light and dark icon sheets, derived from
  [tangrams/icons](https://github.com/tangrams/icons), under this licence:
