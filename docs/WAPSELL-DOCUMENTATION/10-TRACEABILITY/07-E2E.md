## Candidate E2E scenarios implied by verified AS-IS behaviour
These are **hypotheses for validation**, not requirements. They exist because the AS-IS flow
already forces the behaviours, which makes them the cheapest place to detect a broken migration.

| # | Hypothesis | Forced by | Decision backing |
